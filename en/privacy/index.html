3. Contact by email or phone

If you contact me by email or phone, the information you provide is processed only as far as necessary to respond to your request and continue the communication.

Where your request concerns steps prior to a contract or employment relationship, processing is based on the applicable contractual or employment-related legal basis. Other enquiries are processed under Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR on the basis of the legitimate interest in responding to your request.

Recipients may include the email and telecommunications providers used for communication.

This website currently uses no first-party cookies, Local Storage or Session Storage for tracking or analytics. No web analytics or advertising tracking service is integrated.

If analytics is added later, this privacy information will be updated before activation and consent will be obtained where legally required.
