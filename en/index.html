02 / Profile

My professional path began in music. As a trumpeter, section leader and founder of an eight-person brass band, I learned to work precisely, take responsibility, coordinate people and deliver reliably under pressure.

Over time I expanded my toolkit with digital technologies, web fundamentals and AI-assisted ways of working. I now use these tools to understand information faster, structure complex tasks, develop ideas and improve results through iteration.

For me, technology does not replace experience or independent thinking. It expands what I can learn, develop and put into practice on my own.
